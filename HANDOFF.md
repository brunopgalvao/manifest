# Manifest: handoff

Read this first, then `spec/mx.md`, then `bench/results.md`.

## 1. The idea

An agent should put things on a screen and see what is there through one text tree, cheaply and losslessly, instead of generating HTML it cannot read back and reading screenshots that cost more and say less. Manifest is that tree (MX) and the engine that renders it, takes input, and answers perception in the same text with its own layout boxes and diagnostics. The owner's goals, in their words: a native visualizer faster than HTML and JavaScript, visuals that materialize, serve and dissolve, a model that reads the UI back losslessly, a representation efficient and deterministic enough that model makers would adopt it, and the ability to paint anything on the screen, surpassing HTML, with semantics on whatever is painted.

## 2. Decisions that shaped it

| Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Faster than HTML? | Fewer tokens to write and to read back, measured | Parsing is cheap; generation and perception tokens are the cost. |
| Improve vision? | Skip it for your own UI: the renderer knows the tree | Pixels remain for software not built with Manifest and for `look`. |
| A new standard? | Compatible with A2UI, plus perception and lifecycle | A2UI covers the write side; nobody covers read-back and lifecycle. `docs/a2ui-extension.md` proposes both as v1.0 extensions. |
| Two layers, semantic and paint? | One layer: roles with defaults, and any node may take geometry and paint children | A semantic node is a painted node whose paint the renderer supplies. What differs is who supplies the paint and the layout, not the kind of node. |
| A DOM renderer, a TypeScript core, a Python reference? | Retired. One engine in Rust, run natively or as WebAssembly | Three implementations of one format were parity-tested against each other for a while; the engine passed every gate, so it is the only one now. Their recorded outputs stay as test fixtures. |
| Versions of the format? | None. The spec describes the current format | Nobody is using it yet; a clean description beats a migration guide. |

## 3. What exists (October 20, 2026)

- **`spec/mx.md`**: the format. Roles with defaults; geometry on any node (sized nodes are coordinate spaces, positioned nodes sit in them, a positioned node without children gets its default look, with children it shows them); primitives; style words and colours; `series` buffers with bindings, `each` instancing and `anim:` tweens; lifecycle; patches; events; perception at levels 0 to 3 with diagnostics; MCP transport; fidelity tiers.
- **`engine/`** (Cargo workspace): `mx` (parser, ids, patches, diff, perception; `tests/fixtures.json` is the recorded ground truth for the everyday corpus), `render` (taffy layout, tiny-skia paint at device scale, Inter bundled, `space.rs` for geometry, `host.rs` for surfaces, lifecycle, input, tweens and screen composition, `wasm.rs` the plain C ABI; `tests/dom-boxes/` are Chromium's recorded boxes for the parity gate), `mcp` (`manifest-mcp`: stdio JSON-RPC with the five tools, a winit window or `--headless`). `cargo test --release` runs parity, host, stdio, paint and fidelity tests.
- **`web/`**: `manifest.js` (the JavaScript glue, browser and Node), `playground.html` (the engine in the browser), `build.mjs` (the site into `web/dist`), `check.mjs` (drives the playground in Chromium: render, click a tab and a toggle, type into a field, `since=`).
- **`import/`**: `import.ts` captures a rendered page in Chromium and writes MX, semantics first (headings, text, buttons, links, inputs, tables, tabs with panels, metrics by shape, SVG box-and-arrow diagrams as `flow`), then paint (inline SVG as primitives with transforms baked in, `role=img` subtrees as a sized region of rects and text). `score.ts` parses with the engine's wasm build and reports recall, text runs and the round trip of boxes. Nine tests on the HTML twins.
- **`bench/`**: the corpus (twelve visuals; the everyday six in MX, HTML, A2UI v0.9 and v1.0 and A2UI's inference formats; the paint-heavy six in MX, HTML+SVG and A2UI over a paint catalog, generated by `a2ui_v2.mjs` from the engine's tree), the counters (`count_claude.py`, `count_formats.py`, `count_v2.py`, `count_import.py`, all through `count_tokens` with a cache in `out/`), the reader method (`legend.txt`, `bundle.py`, `questions*.json`, `grade*.py`, answers and bundles in `out/acc/`), the fidelity references (`fidelity/`), the defect corpus results, and `results.md`.
- **Site**: built by the Pages workflow from `web/build.mjs` after building the wasm; CI runs the engine tests and the importer tests.

## 4. Results to remember

Everyday six: 788 tokens to create (A2UI Atom 1,762, HTML 4,521), 238 for sixteen edits, 876 to read back at level 1 with 30 of 30 questions right (cropped screenshots 1,544 and 27, accessibility tree 1,415 and 22). Paint-heavy six: 2,661 (HTML+SVG+ARIA 6,104, A2UI paint catalog 6,685); level 1 readers miss the questions that hinge on unlabelled arrows, level 3 readers do not. Fidelity tiers 1 to 3 pass. The engine matches Chromium's layout within 2 px on all 49 recorded boxes. Importer: 37 of 40 reference nodes, 86 of 86 text runs, 1,158 MX tokens for 4,569 of HTML.

- **`bench/pages/`**: real pages under permissive licences imported into MX by `import/src/batch.ts` with `--crawl` (page record: MX, HTML snapshot, screenshot, captured tree, scores, and the trainable pair: the engine's painting of the MX and its level 2 read-back; `report.py` summarises; a nightly workflow reruns it). 246 of 246 parse; text kept 99% median; the median page is a tenth of its HTML. The reader experiment (`pages/questions.json`, `bundle.py`, `grade.py`): 24 questions over 8 pages, MX at level 1 24 and 24 of 24 at 12,102 tokens, screenshots 23 and 24 of 24 at 12,288. This is the dataset lever: the owner chose "volume" as the direction.

## 5. Next

1. **Grow the pages set** to thousands: more licensed sources and a deeper crawl (the nightly workflow runs with `--crawl 10`; raise it and add sources). Keep the licence column honest.
2. **Importer quality on real pages:** icons without names or known shapes still vanish at level 1 (the signature table covers check, cross, star and chevrons); controls the capture does not open (menus, selects) lose text; read chart values from `aria-label` sentences and recognise div-drawn bar charts as `bar`; `textbox` roles on `contenteditable`.
3. **Buffers over records** so `each` can instance rows with several fields; today a buffer is key:value numbers.
4. **Writing rule in the loop:** the engine could flag a `path` or `line` without a label inside a labelled region as `!unlabelled`, since level 1 readers lose it.
5. **Window:** text selection; the dismiss cross is the only chrome.
6. **A2UI upstream:** open the proposal as a discussion in `a2ui-project/a2ui` (it is ready; the owner reviews the text first), then a PR adding MX as an inference format.
7. **Naming, trademark and domain** before anything is announced; `manifest.dev` in the benchmark catalog URL is a placeholder.

## 6. Owner preferences that apply
